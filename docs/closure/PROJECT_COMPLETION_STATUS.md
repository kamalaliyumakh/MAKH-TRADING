## Decision

Local artifacts may be retained as an engineering baseline. They must not be represented as a production-ready trading platform, completed project, approved paper service, controlled-live pilot, investment product or source of expected returns.

## Re-entry conditions

Resume through the normal WBS gates. Confirm named owners/capacity, update stale assumptions and vendor/legal facts, rebaseline schedule/budget, select the next dependency-ready package, validate the current toolchain and rerun risk-proportionate tests. Closure may be reconsidered only after all mandatory acceptance evidence and signatures exist.
