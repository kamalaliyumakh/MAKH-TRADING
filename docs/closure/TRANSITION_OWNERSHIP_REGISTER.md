# WBS 9.6 — Transition Ownership Register

Date prepared: 14 August 2026  
Status: Proposed and unsigned

| Area | Current evidence state | Required accountable owner | Required transition action | Acceptance state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Governance and scope | plans/decision templates exist; approvals and named roles incomplete | Sponsor / Project Manager / Product Owner | confirm roles, scope, funding, schedule and gate authority | Pending |
| Legal, privacy and records | counsel-ready matrices/templates; facts/opinions/signatures absent | Legal/Compliance / Privacy Owner | confirm entity/jurisdictions and obtain qualified dispositions | Pending |
| Broker and providers | neutral contracts/synthetic adapters; suppliers and rights unapproved | Broker Owner / Data Owner | select eligible providers, execute terms and certify adapters/rights | Pending |
| Architecture and engineering | local Angular/.NET/PostgreSQL design and tests; multiple gates unsigned | Engineering Lead / Solution Architect | accept architecture, repositories, build, dependency and integration backlog | Pending |
| Identity and security | provider-neutral controls; provider/independent assurance absent | Security Lead | certify identity/MFA/session, secrets and independent test findings | Pending |
| Data/scanner/evidence | deterministic synthetic slices; persistence/licensed replay incomplete | Data Owner / Product Owner | implement authoritative persistence/providers and certify quality/provenance | Pending |
| Risk, OMS and reconciliation | synthetic/in-memory safety logic; integrations/drills incomplete | Risk Owner / Engineering Lead | approve policies and complete durable broker/reconciliation testing | Pending |
| UX/accessibility | code-native flows and local checks; representative/AT acceptance incomplete | Product Owner / Accessibility Lead | perform usability, accessibility and supported-browser acceptance | Pending |
| Operations and recovery | runbooks/evaluators exist; environment/telemetry/on-call/drills absent | Operations Owner | deploy approved stack, assign support and execute recovery/incident drills | Pending |
| Paper and controlled live | protocols/templates exist; no certified paper operation or live pilot | Sponsor / Product / Risk / Operations | remain no-go until all predecessor evidence and decisions are signed | Pending |
| Records and repository custody | artifacts in local workspace; no approved archival/retention owner | Project Manager / Records Owner | approve repository, evidence archive, retention, access and backup custody | Pending |

No row is transferred until named individuals accept through durable records. A role label or this template alone does not transfer responsibility.
