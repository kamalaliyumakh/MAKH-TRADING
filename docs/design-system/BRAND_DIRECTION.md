# MAKH Trading Platform — Brand Direction

Last updated: 20 August 2026

## Positioning

MAKH is a risk-controlled decision and execution workspace for self-directed traders. It helps users discover candidates, verify evidence, evaluate risk, submit authorized orders and reconstruct outcomes. It does not promise returns, remove market risk or replace regulated broker and professional advice.

## Brand idea

**Calm precision under pressure.**

The experience should feel like an instrument panel: disciplined, legible, evidence-rich and restrained. Opportunity is visible, but safety state and uncertainty receive equal or greater visual weight.

## Personality

| Trait | Express as | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Precise | explicit units, sources, timestamps and states | vague “hot”, “winning” or “sure” labels |
| Calm | stable layouts, restrained motion, measured color | flashing prices, confetti, countdown pressure |
| Protective | risk capacity, blocks and consequences before action | burying limits or encouraging overrides |
| Transparent | provenance, rule results and broker uncertainty | hiding data age or implying execution certainty |
| Professional | concise plain language and consistent hierarchy | jargon for its own sake or casino aesthetics |

## Voice and terminology

- Use direct, neutral verbs: Review, Verify, Create proposal, Submit paper order, Request cancellation, Block entries.
- State what is known: “Broker acknowledgement pending” rather than “Order failed” after a timeout.
- State consequence before destructive confirmation.
- Pair market values with units and timestamps.
- Never use “guaranteed”, “risk-free”, “easy profit”, “winner” or certainty-based recommendations.
- Treat 100%–600% gain as “Extreme move — elevated liquidity/halt risk; not a buy signal.”

## Visual signature

- Deep navy establishes trust and anchors navigation.
- Orange (`#FF6A16`) is the primary brand and interaction accent; it does not mean “buy”.
- Deep surfaces use near-black navy, layered blue-grey panels and restrained orange atmospheric gradients.
- Teal communicates verified/healthy states.
- Amber communicates attention, stale/degraded state and caution.
- Red is reserved for hard blocks, destructive actions and critical failures—not ordinary negative P&L alone.
- Paper and Live use text, icon and pattern/border differences in addition to color.
- Public marketing uses generous typography and cinematic negative space; authenticated workspaces use compact cards, tables and a persistent desktop navigation rail.
- Tables and cards use subtle blue-grey borders and shallow tonal depth; decorative glow is limited to the public brand atmosphere and never applied to live values.

## Logo direction

The user-supplied orange rising-bar/arrow artwork is the approved source identity for this implementation. Headers pair its orange mark with a high-contrast `MAKH` / `TRADING` text lockup so the dark navy lettering in the source image remains legible on dark surfaces. The upward geometry is a brand exception and must never be presented beside language that promises performance or implies a recommendation.

The original asset is retained as `makh trading logo.png`; application copies are served as `makh-trading-logo.png`. The header treatment crops the supplied mark non-destructively in CSS and renders the exact wordmark in HTML for responsive clarity.

## Reference-led interface direction

- Public landing: dark hero, oversized white value proposition, orange CTA, synthetic market-preview card and prominent risk boundary.
- Portal: fixed 15rem desktop navigation rail, compact analytical surfaces, orange section hierarchy and green limited to positive/healthy data states.
- Mobile: navigation becomes a horizontal top region; content collapses to one column without document-level horizontal overflow.
- Safety: reference-image order buttons, live balances, kill-switch controls and account actions are visual inspiration only and remain excluded until separately authorized.

## Content hierarchy

1. Safety and system state.
2. Task/page title and scope.
3. Evidence and decision inputs.
4. Primary action.
5. Secondary analysis and history.

Profit/loss color never overrides readability, signs and explicit labels. Green is not used as a standalone invitation to buy.
