## Calculation and evidence rules

- Provider calculators are estimates, not quotes. Free tiers and promotional credits cannot be assumed in the approved baseline.
- Recurring, one-time, tax, contingency, market-data pass-through, egress, support and recovery-exercise costs remain separate.
- A missing required input makes the affected scenario unavailable; it must not be represented as zero.
- No monetary total is fabricated while BI-01–BI-08 remain Pending.
