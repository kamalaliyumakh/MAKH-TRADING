## Capacity and conflict rules

- Capacity is recorded as a time-bounded commitment, not an estimate inferred from activity.
- Primary and backup coverage must identify unavailable periods and escalation coverage.
- Sponsor, Product, Risk, Security, Operations, qualified legal/privacy and final acceptance decisions cannot be self-approved by the person producing the underlying evidence.
- One person may hold multiple roles only when conflicts are recorded and an independent reviewer is assigned for each consequential decision.
- High-risk handoffs require durable evidence and receiving-owner acceptance; team chat alone is insufficient.
- Missing, stale, conflicting or revoked assignment, capacity, authority or access evidence preserves Pending.
