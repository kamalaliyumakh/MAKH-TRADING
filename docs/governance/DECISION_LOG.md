# MAKH Trading Platform — Decision Log

Last updated: 12 August 2026

## Rules

- Use `DEC-###` for governance/product/vendor decisions and ADRs for architecture decisions.
- Record the accountable decision-maker, options considered, rationale, consequences and evidence.
- A proposed or pending entry is not approval.
- Superseded decisions link to their replacement and remain auditable.
