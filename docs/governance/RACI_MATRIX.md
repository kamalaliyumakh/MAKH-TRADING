## RACI controls

- Each row has one accountable role. `A/R` is allowed when the same role owns and performs the activity.
- Named people may hold multiple role codes, but high-risk self-approval conflicts are disclosed and compensated.
- “Consulted” is required before the decision, not retrospective notification.
- Absence or silence is not approval.
