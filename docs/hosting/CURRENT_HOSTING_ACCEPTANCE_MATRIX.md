## Evidence rule

Provider documentation, local emulation, infrastructure templates, successful compilation and calculator estimates do not constitute an HOA result. A future result must bind the provider, account, region/zone, service/tier/version, topology, configuration digest, workload/data set, start/end time, expected outcome, measured outcome, raw evidence digest, defects and accountable acceptance.
