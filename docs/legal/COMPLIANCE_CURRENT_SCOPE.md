## Evidence and decision controls

- CE-001 through CE-016 must retain exact version, date, custodian and durable reference; missing or template evidence cannot be treated as approval.
- A legal conclusion may be entered only by the identified qualified reviewer within a defined jurisdiction, facts, product scope and effective date.
- Every condition requires an owner, due date, validation method and release effect. Conflicting or superseded evidence remains traceable.
- Privacy and records approvals remain distinct from product, security, operational and launch approvals.
- Material changes to users, jurisdictions, compensation, recommendations, broker/order flow, providers, personal data or claims trigger re-review.
- The WBS 8.7 operational decision is Reject/Defer and WBS 9.1 independent testing is unexecuted; neither predecessor can be inferred as accepted.
