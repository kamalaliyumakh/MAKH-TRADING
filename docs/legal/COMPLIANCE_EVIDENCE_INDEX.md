## Index controls

- Replace “Missing” or “Template only” only when a durable artifact has been reviewed.
- Record the exact version, date and repository/data-room reference; do not paste secrets or sensitive identifiers.
- A condition must have an owner, due date, validation method and release effect.
- Conflicting or superseded evidence must remain traceable.
- Only the qualified reviewer may enter a legal conclusion; only accountable owners may sign acceptance.
