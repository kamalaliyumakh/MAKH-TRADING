# WBS 9.2 — Compliance Evidence Readiness Plan

Last updated: 13 August 2026  
Status: Preparation only — no qualified legal/compliance approval

## Objective and boundary

Prepare an auditable evidence package for qualified reviewers to decide whether the proposed owner-only, paper-first platform may progress toward a controlled-live pilot. This plan organizes existing questions and required evidence; it does not answer jurisdiction-specific legal questions and is not legal advice.

No public launch, external user access, provider purchase, production personal data, broker credentials, order transmission or live trading is authorized by this package.

## Entry prerequisites for qualified review

1. LF-01–LF-12 operating facts are answered by accountable owners with durable evidence.
2. The legal entity, controlling persons, operating jurisdictions, server/support locations and intended user population are confirmed.
3. Proposed broker, identity, hosting and data providers are identified with contracting entities and service regions.
4. Product functionality, compensation, personalization, discretion, order flow, custody and marketing claims are frozen for the review version.
5. A qualified reviewer is named with jurisdiction, professional capacity, scope, conflicts check and authority recorded.
6. The evidence data room is access-controlled, versioned and contains no unapproved credentials or unnecessary personal data.

## Evidence quality rules

- Every assertion must identify evidence ID, owner, version/as-of date, source or authority, location, reviewer and disposition.
- Primary law/regulator material supports issue identification; a qualified opinion records the project-specific conclusion.
- Vendor and broker permissions require executed or formally approved contractual evidence, not marketing pages.
- “Pending,” “not applicable,” conditions, expiry and review triggers must be explicit. Silence is not approval.
- Superseded evidence remains immutable and linked to the replacing version.
- Secrets, account numbers and unnecessary personal data are prohibited from the evidence index.

## Exit criteria

WBS 9.2 may move beyond preparation only when:

1. all LF-01–LF-12 facts are answered and evidenced;
2. every LC-01–LC-16 item has a qualified disposition;
3. all PR-01–PR-12 and RR-01–RR-08 items are approved, conditionally approved with controlled owners/dates, or explicitly not applicable with rationale;
4. required opinions, contracts, notices, schedules and responsibility matrices have durable references;
5. Critical/High legal, privacy, broker, data-rights and records blockers are closed;
6. Legal/Compliance, Privacy, Product and Sponsor approval records are signed; and
7. controlled-live remains separately subject to security, risk, operations and go/no-go gates.
