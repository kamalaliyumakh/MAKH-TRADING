## Fail-closed product constraints

1. Local development and synthetic/paper evaluation only until entity, jurisdiction and user population are confirmed.
2. No public launch, paid access, referral economics, third-party onboarding or performance marketing before qualified approval.
3. No representation that MAKH is an adviser, broker, fiduciary, exchange, guaranteed system or regulator-approved service.
4. No custody, pooled funds, discretionary third-party trading or order transmission for another person.
5. No broker/provider contract, production entitlement, live credential or controlled-live activity before eligibility, rights and legal/privacy duties are approved.
6. Screening signals must remain distinct from suitability or future-performance claims.
7. Broker and regulated-provider records remain authoritative for eligibility, restrictions, settlement and order availability.
8. Missing, stale, conflicting or changed facts trigger qualified re-review and preserve the restrictive state.

## Authority boundary

This scope does not answer an operating fact, interpret law, determine classification, establish an exemption, approve privacy or records controls, select or contract a broker/provider, publish a service, solicit funds, provide advice, authorize paper trading, allocate capital or create pilot/live authority.
