## Source-control rules

- Re-open the official source at the time of every material decision; this log is not a substitute for the source.
- Record source title, URL, review date, version/effective date and the exact product fact being assessed.
- A changed Act, rule, notice, guideline, operator status, product feature, jurisdiction, user population, compensation model, provider or data flow triggers re-review.
- Search-result summaries, third-party articles and Codex analysis cannot establish legal authority.
