# MAKH Trading Platform — Disclosure Copy Draft

Last updated: 12 August 2026  
Status: Draft for qualified legal/compliance review; not approved for publication

## Global product notice

MAKH is a decision-support and order-management tool for self-directed trading. Screening results, rankings, news, social-attention indicators and risk calculations are informational outputs based on configured rules and available data. They are not guarantees of accuracy, liquidity, execution or future performance and are not, by themselves, instructions to buy or sell a security.

## Trading-risk notice

Trading low-priced, low-float and rapidly moving securities involves substantial risk, including volatility, wide spreads, trading halts, limited liquidity, slippage and loss of the entire amount committed. A reported gain between 100% and 600% indicates exceptional movement and heightened risk; it does not predict continued appreciation.

## Data notice

Quotes, float, volume, news, filings and social indicators may be delayed, corrected, incomplete or defined differently by each provider. The platform must display source, timestamp, freshness and confidence where available. Users must review the current executable quote and broker status before acting.

## Social-signal notice

Social activity may be inaccurate, manipulated or unrelated to issuer fundamentals. It cannot verify a catalyst or authorize an order. Material claims should be checked against higher-trust issuer, exchange or regulatory sources.

## Paper-mode notice

Paper trading is a simulation. Simulated fills, liquidity, fees and slippage may differ materially from live execution. Paper results do not represent actual performance and do not authorize live trading.

## Broker notice

Orders are submitted only through the user's approved broker account. The broker controls account eligibility, buying power, margin, order acceptance, execution, restrictions and final records. Platform state may be delayed; uncertain or unreconciled broker state blocks new exposure.

## Performance and marketing rules

- Never use “guaranteed,” “risk-free,” “approved by the SEC,” or equivalent language.
- Do not publish selected wins without a fair, substantiated and approved presentation of losses, assumptions, periods, fees and limitations.
- Label hypothetical, backtested and simulated results clearly and separately from live results.
- Do not use testimonials, endorsements or affiliate claims before conflict, compensation and marketing review.
- Do not imply that platform risk controls prevent loss.

## Consent presentation

Final approved terms, privacy and risk notices must be versioned, accessible before acceptance and recorded with user, document/version, timestamp and evidence hash. Material revisions require renewed acknowledgement when counsel determines it is necessary.
