## Local readiness verification

Run `node eng/verify-legal-discovery-readiness.mjs` to validate the counsel-ready package and write `outputs/legal-discovery-readiness/readiness-manifest.json`.

The verifier checks seven primary-source findings, LF-01–LF-12, seven provisional launch constraints, LC-01–LC-16, PR-01–PR-12, RR-01–RR-08, eight disclosure sections, eight Pending acceptance criteria, seven Pending approval fields and the unavailable WBS 1.1 predecessor. It hashes all seven evidence inputs and fails closed if an inventory or authority boundary changes.

Passing local verification means **Prepared / Qualified Review Pending** only. It is not legal advice, a legal conclusion, approval of privacy or records controls, authorization to contract with a broker or provider, permission to publish, or paper/live trading authority.

WBS 1.2 remains in progress until the operating facts are confirmed and a qualified reviewer supplies the jurisdiction-specific conclusion. Codex research is not legal advice or acceptance.
