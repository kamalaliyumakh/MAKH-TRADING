## Mandatory constraints

- No live identity, credential, account, endpoint, broker order or capital.
- No target, tenant, provider or network outside the signed inventory.
- No uncontrolled availability test, social engineering, persistence, destructive change or sensitive-data extraction unless separately and explicitly authorized.
- Entry lock and monitoring remain active. A critical control failure pauses testing and starts incident handling.
- Findings and evidence use redacted identifiers and approved restricted storage.
