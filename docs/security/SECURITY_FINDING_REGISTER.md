## Rules

- Finding IDs are immutable; corrections append a new version/history record.
- Critical/high findings block release until remediated, independently retested and closed by authorized Security/Sponsor owners.
- “Not exploitable,” duplicate, accepted or out-of-scope dispositions require evidence and accountable approval.
- Do not store credentials, full tokens, unnecessary personal/account data or weaponized exploit material in this register.
- Absence of a finding is not evidence of a pass when the test has not run.
